I wanted to share some tips that'll help you get even more from your board:


1. CREATE CUSTOM COLUMNS

Don't settle for just To Do / In Progress / Done!

Create columns that match YOUR workflow:
- Backlog → This Week → Today → Done
- Ideas → Planning → Building → Shipped
- Low Priority → Medium → High → Completed

Click the "Add Column" button and customize away.


2. USE DESCRIPTIONS FOR CONTEXT

Each task can have a description. Click the card to edit.

Great for:
- Adding quick notes
- Listing subtasks
- Including links or references
- Clarifying unclear titles

Future you will thank present you.


3. LIMIT YOUR "IN PROGRESS"

Try this: Never have more than 3 tasks in your "In Progress" column.

Why? It forces you to finish things instead of starting everything.

The result? You'll complete more and feel less overwhelmed.


4. KEYBOARD POWER USER TIP

You can navigate FlowBoard with your keyboard!

Try dragging with keyboard shortcuts for even faster organizing.


5. SCREENSHOT YOUR WINS

## Email Sending Strategy

### Frequency
- Welcome: Immediately upon signup/first use
- Tips: 1 week after first use
- Newsletter: Monthly
- Feature announcements: As needed (max 1 per week)
- Re-engagement: 30 days inactive

### Best Practices

1. **Subject Lines**
   - Keep under 50 characters
   - Test multiple options
   - Avoid spammy words (FREE, LIMITED TIME, etc.)
   - Use emojis sparingly (1 max)

2. **Email Length**
   - Keep under 300 words when possible
   - Use formatting (bold, lists) for scannability
   - One clear CTA per email

3. **Personalization**
   - Use first name if available
   - Reference user behavior (if privacy-compliant)
   - Segment by user type when possible

4. **Deliverability**
   - Authenticate domain (SPF, DKIM)
   - Clean list regularly
   - Honor unsubscribes immediately
   - Avoid spam triggers

5. **Testing**
   - A/B test subject lines
   - Test on mobile devices
   - Check all links
   - Preview in multiple email clients

### Metrics to Track
- Open rate (aim for 20-25%)
- Click-through rate (aim for 3-5%)
- Reply rate (quality > quantity)
- Unsubscribe rate (keep under 0.5%)
- Conversion to app usage

### Automation Triggers
- User signs up → Welcome email
- 7 days after first use → Tips email
- 30 days inactive → Re-engagement
- Feature launch → Announcement email
- Monthly → Newsletter

## Legal Requirements

### Required Elements
- Physical mailing address
- Clear unsubscribe mechanism
- Sender identification
- Accurate subject lines
- Honor opt-outs within 10 days

### Privacy Compliance
- GDPR: Get explicit consent, allow data deletion
- CAN-SPAM: Include physical address, clear sender
- CASL (Canada): Express/implied consent required

### Best Practice
- Double opt-in for signups
- Clear privacy policy
- Data retention policy
- Easy preference management

---

Remember: Our emails should reflect FlowBoard's values: simple, honest, privacy-respecting, and helpful. Never pushy, never spammy.
